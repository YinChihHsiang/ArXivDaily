#### Angular Momentum Transport across Accretion States around a rapidly spinning black hole
 - **Authors:** Rohan Raha, Koushik Chatterjee
 - **Subjects:** Subjects:
High Energy Astrophysical Phenomena (astro-ph.HE)
 - **Arxiv link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01827

 - **Pdf link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.01827

 - **Abstract**
 Accretion requires the outward transport of angular momentum, but the relative contributions of matter advection, turbulent stresses, ordered magnetic fields, winds, and black-hole spin remain uncertain in strongly magnetized flows. We use high-resolution three-dimensional GRMHD simulations around a rapidly rotating black hole ($a=0.998$) to compare SANE, intermediate-flux, and magnetically arrested disk states. In SANE and intermediate states, outward stresses are insufficient to overcome inward angular-momentum advection, and the net flux remains inward. In MAD, ordered Maxwell stresses regulate the disk and drive winds, while the Blandford-Znajek jet carries a distinct, near-constant flux extracted from the black hole, making the total angular-momentum flux outward. Magnetic-flux eruptions modulate both channels. During an eruption, an equatorial current sheet extends beyond the ergosphere, and low-density, magnetically dominated bubbles of expelled flux enter the polar region. The jet becomes asymmetric, its tilt more than doubles, and its opening angle first widens and then contracts below its pre-eruption value. Eruptions suppress the horizon accretion rate and briefly reverse the angular-momentum flux through the jet surface, while reorganizing the Maxwell stresses in the disk. As horizon-threading flux re-accumulates, the turbulent stress recovers toward its saturated MAD level. Eruption durations and lags among horizon flux, accretion rate, and jet tilt of ~40-90 $r_g/c$ correspond to milliseconds for stellar-mass black holes and tens of minutes for Sgr A*. These results distinguish disk-regulating magnetic torques from black-hole-sourced jet transport and show how flux eruptions couple disk accretion, winds, and jet morphology.

#### Integrable mass of Kerr-Newman-Bertotti-Robinson black hole
 - **Authors:** Li Hu, Rong-Gen Cai, Shan-Wen Jiang, Shao-Jiang Wang
 - **Subjects:** Subjects:
General Relativity and Quantum Cosmology (gr-qc)
 - **Arxiv link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03123

 - **Pdf link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.03123

 - **Abstract**
 The nontrivial asymptotic structure of rotating black holes in the Bertotti-Robinson universe complicates a direct determination of their conserved mass, leading previous studies to rely on a priori thermodynamic input. We address this long-standing problem using integrability methods, establish local analytic existence and uniqueness via the Cauchy-Kovalevskaja theorem, and obtain a closed expression via a rescaled recurrence whose termination is proved at all orders. The resulting Kerr-Newman-Bertotti-Robinson mass coincides with the expression previously derived using the canonical first law identity and reproduces the masses of both Kerr-Bertotti-Robinson subcases. With the mass thus fixed without invoking thermodynamic relations for the first time, we rigorously establish the first law of black-hole thermodynamics and the Smarr formula for a whole family of Kerr-Bertotti-Robinson black holes suitable for future applications.
