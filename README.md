#### Prompt Jet Launching and Delayed Thermal Disk in Tidal Disruption Event for Prolonged Activity GRB 220706A
 - **Authors:** Yuri Sato, Rin Oikawa
 - **Subjects:** Subjects:
High Energy Astrophysical Phenomena (astro-ph.HE)
 - **Arxiv link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31484

 - **Pdf link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31484

 - **Abstract**
 GRB 220706A exhibits X-ray flaring activity extending to ~51 days after the trigger, along with a luminous optical thermal component emerging on a timescale of ~10^6 s. Such prolonged central-engine activity could be powered by a collapsar fed by an extended stellar envelope. However, a relativistic jet may struggle to break out of the extended envelope. These challenges motivate a tidal disruption event (TDE), although explaining both the early relativistic jet and the luminous late optical thermal emission within one TDE framework remains difficult. We investigate a TDE scenario in which shocks between stellar debris streams drive the early inflow of some debris, potentially carrying preexisting fossil magnetic flux toward the black hole (BH) and powering an initial relativistic jet via the Blandford-Znajek mechanism. The remaining debris circularizes into an accretion disk, whose thermal emission accounts for the late optical thermal component. For GRB 220706A, the optical timescale, luminosity, and color temperature are consistent with disruption of a blue supergiant (M_*~50M_{sun}, R_*~20~R_{sun}) by a BH of mass M_{BH}~10^5 M_{sun}, assuming that the accretion disk forms on a timescale comparable to the orbital period of the most tightly bound stellar debris. A magnetic field strength of ~10^5 G is required near the BH before disruption to power a jet capable of producing the inferred gamma-ray luminosity. Continued fallback may sustain central-engine activity beyond the observed X-ray flaring episode, making future X-ray monitoring a test of this scenario.

#### Ricci-flat Seeds, Trombone Rescaling and Magnetizing Kerr Black Holes
 - **Authors:** Liang Ma, H. Lu
 - **Subjects:** Subjects:
General Relativity and Quantum Cosmology (gr-qc); High Energy Physics - Theory (hep-th)
 - **Arxiv link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30256

 - **Pdf link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.30256

 - **Abstract**
 We present a new family of Ricci-flat rotating metrics, which we refer to as Solution II, obtained by demagnetizing the genuine Kerr-Bertotti-Robinson (G-KBR) black hole. Together with Solution I, which was previously obtained by demagnetizing the Kerr-Bertotti-Robinson (KBR) black hole, this gives two families of Ricci-flat deformations of the Kerr black hole, each labeled by $B$. We study the global structures, thermodynamics, various limits, and the local connections among these limits. We also study the remagnetizations in both Einstein-Maxwell and Kaluza-Klein theories, and find that, to yield a physically acceptable solution, the Ernst transformation should be augmented by an appropriate trombone rescaling.
